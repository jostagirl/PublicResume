# 🛁 PURESPA HEAT + CHEMICAL ONE-SHEET (210 GALLONS)
Updated March 26, 2026

## 🎯 TARGETS
- Free Chlorine (FC): **3–5 ppm**
- After soak spike: **5–8 ppm (temporary)**
- pH: **7.2–7.6**

---

# 🔥 DAILY HEAT SCHEDULE

## 🌙 AFTER USE (~8 PM)
- Add **dichlor dose (see below)**
- Run circulation/jets **20–30 minutes**
- Turn **HEAT OFF**
- Ensure:
  - Thermal blanket ON (water surface)
  - Inflatable disk ON
  - Outer lid ON

👉 Spa sits overnight with **no circulation**

---

## ☀️ NEXT DAY (HEATING BACK UP)

### Turn HEAT ON:
- Small temp drop → **~2 PM**
- Medium drop → **~12–1 PM**
- Large drop → **late morning**

👉 Target temp: **100°F by ~7 PM**

---

## ☀️ BONUS (Sunny Days)
- 12–3 PM:
  - Lid OFF  
  - Thermal blanket ON  
- Then heat later (~2–3 PM)

---

## 🌆 USE (~7 PM)
- Confirm temp ~100°F
- Use spa
- Repeat cycle

---

# 🧪 CHEMICAL ROUTINE

## 🔹 DICHLOR (PRIMARY CONTROL)

### After EVERY soak:
- **½ teaspoon**
- Circulate **20–30 minutes**

👉 Raises ~3–5 ppm chlorine

---

### Heavy use (2+ people / long soak):
- **¾ to 1 teaspoon**

---

### Weekly shock (or if water is off):
- **1.5 to 2 teaspoons**
- Circulate **30–45 minutes**

---

## 🔹 TRICHLOR TABS (BACKGROUND ONLY)

### Use:
- Break into **¼ tab**
- Place in floater

---

### Goal:
- Maintain **~1–2 ppm baseline chlorine**

---

### Important:
- ❌ Do NOT use full tabs  
- ❌ Do NOT rely on tabs alone  
- ✔ Supplement with dichlor always  

---

# ⚖️ HOW THEY WORK TOGETHER (WITH DOSAGES)

| Situation        | Chemical | Amount (210 gal spa) | Notes |
|-----------------|----------|---------------------|-------|
| After soak      | Dichlor  | ½ tsp               | Standard dose (~3–5 ppm) |
| Heavy use       | Dichlor  | ¾–1 tsp             | 2+ people or long soak |
| Water dull      | Dichlor  | 1 tsp               | Light correction boost |
| Weekly reset    | Dichlor  | 1.5–2 tsp           | Full shock |
| Daily baseline  | Trichlor | ¼ tab (in floater)  | Maintains ~1–2 ppm |

# 📅 WEEKLY MAINTENANCE

## 1–2x per week:
- Check:
  - Chlorine level (never zero)
  - pH (7.2–7.6)

---

## Weekly:
- Rinse filter with hose

---

# 🧠 OPERATING RULES

## Rule 1 — No 24/7 heat mode
👉 Avoid constant pump runtime

---

## Rule 2 — Never let chlorine hit zero
👉 Most important rule

---

## Rule 3 — Always circulate after dosing
👉 20–30 min minimum

---

## Rule 4 — Tabs are supplemental only
👉 Dichlor does the real work

---

## Rule 5 — Blanket always on when not in use

Layer order:
1. Water  
2. Thermal blanket  
3. Inflatable disk  
4. Outer lid  

---

# ⚠️ TROUBLE GUIDE

### Cloudy water:
- Add **1–2 tsp dichlor**
- Circulate 30–45 min

---

### Strong chlorine smell:
- Add **dichlor (shock)**
👉 Smell = not enough active chlorine

---

### Slimy / off feel:
- **Shock immediately (2 tsp)**
- Check filter

---

# 💡 EFFICIENCY CHEATS

- Heat mostly **before 4 PM (PG&E peak)**
- Target **100°F (not 102°F)**
- Use sun when possible
- Keep spa sealed tightly

---

# 🧭 SIMPLE DAILY FLOW

- Soak → dichlor → circulate → OFF  
- Next day → heat → soak → repeat  

---

# 🧱 BOTTOM LINE

👉 Scheduled heating + controlled sanitation  
👉 Lower power use + stable water  

This system works **as long as you stay consistent with post-soak dosing**
